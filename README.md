# ComfyUI-VideoExtend

Nodes for extending a video at its start and/or end with clips from any video model, while keeping the original
clip's own frames, size, frame rate and sound. Built for the Silverside **Video Extend** workflow. ComfyUI 0.45+; no
extra dependencies (PyAV, numpy, Pillow and torch ship with ComfyUI; join smoothing also uses OpenCV when the
install has it, as most do, and without it smooths colour and repeated frames only). Model-agnostic: nothing here knows which model
made the clips.

| Node | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Video Edge Frames** (`VideoExtendEdgeFrames`) | The clip's first and last frames (IMAGE), plus width, height, fps, frames, seconds and whether it has sound. Streams the clip once; no frames are held. For image-to-video models that start or end on a given frame. |
| **Extend Segment (Head / Tail)** (`VideoExtendSegment`) | The first (`head`) or last (`tail`) N seconds of a clip, optionally played backwards (sound dropped). What a model that continues a clip needs to see; `head` + `reverse` lets a forward-only model make what comes before the clip. Optional `pad`: the frames are centred on the nearest 16:9 (landscape, square) or 9:16 (portrait) canvas with blurred, darkened bars (black bars made Gemini cut away), for a model that only makes those shapes (it then has nothing to crop; Extend Join's centred cover-crop removes the bars again). Outputs the part and its length. |
| **Extend Join** (`VideoExtendJoin`) | `before` parts + source + `after` parts as one clip (H.264 crf 12, yuv420p). Each part is matched to the source's frame rate by time and scaled to cover its size (Lanczos, centre crop: no stretching, no borders), so a part generated at 720p comes back at the source's 1080p. The source's frames and sound stay as they are; each part keeps its own sound with 80 ms fades at the joins (silence when it has none, or when the before side was made reversed). Up to 3 parts per side, lazy: only the parts in use run. `drop_before_last` / `drop_after_first` drop frames where a part repeats the source's own edge frame (image-to-video from the source's last frame); `max_seconds` caps each side (the far end is cut). Optional `match_speed`: each side is played at the source's speed (camera and in-frame motion measured over 2 s on each side of the join), at a rate of 0.67 to 1.6 and, with `max_seconds`, never more than the footage allows (generate extra seconds to speed up into); overlap copies stay at their own speed, the parts' sound is not retimed, and smoothing's own speed step is off. When an overlap's copy does not line up with the source (its frames away from the last few differ by more than 5 grey levels on average, e.g. a model that cropped or zoomed), that join is cut and smoothed instead of dissolved; the report's `handover` says which. Outputs the video, a JSON report and `seams`: the frame index of every join (between parts, and between a part and the source). Optional: `overlap_before` / `overlap_after` (seconds): each part starts, toward the source, with its own copy of that much of the piece next to it (a model that returns its input + the new footage, sliced that much early), and the two are cross-dissolved there instead of cut. `smooth_joins`: every join with the source that is cut is smoothed on the generated side only (extend_smooth.py): repeated frames dropped, the part's camera motion ramped in from the source's over 1 s (a thin mirrored edge of up to 1.2% may open, beyond that a slowly growing zoom-in covers it), speed eased, a flow pull toward the source's edge frame where the flow is consistent, the colour matched (overall offset kept, local remainder fading over 1 s) and, when the part is softer than the source, a matched unsharp mask over the whole part; the source's frames are copied from the source file; the camera, speed and flow steps switch off on fast motion. The sound stays in sync: a part that gets shorter or longer loses or gains sound at its far end. Both off = exactly the earlier behaviour. |
| **Extend Auto Resolution** (`VideoExtendAutoResolution`) | `480p`, `720p` or `1080p` from the clip's short side (up to 600 px, up to 900, larger), for the generation resolution, the short side itself, and `aspect_16_9`: `16:9` for a landscape or square clip, `9:16` for a portrait one (the canvas Extend Segment's `pad` uses). |
| **Extend Pad Image (16:9 / 9:16)** (`VideoExtendPadImage`) | An image on the same canvas as Extend Segment's `pad` (blurred bars), for start and end frames of a model that only makes 16:9 or 9:16; also outputs which shape. |
| **Extend Opening Guide** (`VideoExtendOpeningGuide`) | The frame the camera saw `seconds` before the clip: its first frame shifted and scaled back along the camera move measured in its first second (at most 0.6 frame widths, at most about a third of the frame left to fill; the rest blurred edges), for an image model to complete as the opening frame of a lead-in. Only when the camera move is the action (the camera moves, the frame's own motion is slow); otherwise the first frame unchanged and `used` false. |
| **Extend Edge Motion** (`VideoExtendEdgeMotion`) | How fast the clip moves in its first and last `seconds` (default 1), measured with optical flow (OpenCV; empty when it is missing): the camera's speed, direction and zoom, and how fast things move in the frame against it, in frame widths per second and in words (still, slow, moderate, fast, very fast). One sentence for a prompt writer, so footage generated at a join keeps the clip's pace (an LLM watching a clip at about one frame per second cannot see speed), plus a JSON report. |
| **Extend Seam Windows** (`VideoExtendSeamWindows`) | Measures each join's jump (frame change across the join over the median change of the 40 frames around it: about 1 = invisible, 2 = a clear jump) and, for joins above `threshold` (`mode` auto; `always`, `off`), cuts a window of `2 x context + gap` frames (9 + 23 + 9 = 41, 8n + 1 for LTX) at about `pixels` area, keeping the clip's exact shape (scaled, then padded right and bottom to multiples of 32 by repeating the edge pixels). Outputs the windows as a list, so the nodes after it (a model that regenerates the gap from guides on both sides) run once per join, plus their starts, `any`, size, frame count, fps and where the second guide goes. |
| **Extend Seam Splice** (`VideoExtendSeamSplice`) | Puts the regenerated windows back, mapped exactly (padding dropped, scaled to the video's size): the gap replaces the original, and the window fades in and out over the guide frames on each side, where the model reproduces the original, so no double image shows in the gap. Lazy: when no join needs repair, the model before it never runs. Sound and every other frame stay as they are. |

Offline use (no ComfyUI): `python extend_core.py probe SRC`, `edges SRC OUTPREFIX`,
`segment SRC OUT --part head --seconds 10 --reverse`,
`join SRC OUT --before B1 --after A1 A2 [--before-reversed] [--drop-after-first 1] [--max-seconds 5]`.
